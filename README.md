**Automation loops for the real world.**

HotLoop is an industrial-grade automation platform for connecting sensors, controls, and devices into reliable, closed-loop automations — built to run anywhere from a single home to an industrial floor.

## Two ways to run it

**At home, free.** Anyone can run HotLoop on their own hardware for personal, household, or hobbyist use — home automation, personal projects, tinkering — at no cost, under the [HotLoop Community License](https://github.com/HotLoop-io/HotLoop-io/blob/main/LICENSE).

**In a business, through Embernet.** HotLoop is proud to partner with [Embernet](https://embernet.ai) as our official Enterprise/Business release. Any commercial or for-profit use of HotLoop — internal or customer-facing — is available exclusively through Embernet's Enterprise offering. This isn't a limited trial or a feature-gated tier; it's how HotLoop is supported, maintained, and warrantied for organizations that depend on it.

See the [license](https://github.com/HotLoop-io/HotLoop-io/blob/main/LICENSE) for the exact terms.
