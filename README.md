# HotLoop-io

The legal and brand home for HotLoop. Three things live here, and each is the only copy that counts.

**The license.** [LICENSE.md](./LICENSE.md) is the Business Source License 1.1 (SPDX `BUSL-1.1`), filled in for HotLoop. It governs **HotLoop IoT, HotLoop Edge, HotLoop Gateway, HotLoop Edge Relay, HotLoop Flow and HotLoop Cascade**, all of them, and it is the same file in every product repo. Home and lab use is free. Business use is free when it runs through [EmberNET](https://embernet.ai), HotLoop's channel for business use, and there is no other business path. Hosting HotLoop as a service, reselling it and embedding it in a product you sell are not granted. Each version converts to GPL-3.0 four years after it is first made publicly available. It is not an open source license until a version converts, and I won't call it one. Copies of earlier releases keep the license they came with. The docs site mirrors this file word for word and its build fails if the mirror drifts, so when the license changes, it changes here first. The plain-language version is on the [org homepage](https://github.com/HotLoop-io) and at [docs.hotloop.io/licensing](https://docs.hotloop.io/licensing/), and the legal text wins every argument with either of them.

**The terms.** [TERMS.md](./TERMS.md) is the HotLoop Terms of Service, published at [hotloop.io/terms](https://hotloop.io/terms). It covers how HotLoop is distributed, what we collect (nothing), what we promise and what we don't. Where it and the license disagree about what you may do with the software, the license wins.

**The brand.** [brand/](./brand) holds the design tokens, the logo files, and the written standard for color, type and voice. `brand/tokens.css` is the one copy of the palette that decides anything. [hotloop.io](https://hotloop.io) and [docs.hotloop.io](https://docs.hotloop.io) each vendor a copy, and each one's build fetches this file and fails if its copy has drifted. So change a color here, then copy it over, or neither site deploys. Anything else carrying a copy keeps it in step by hand, so check it when you change one.

Everything else about HotLoop, the actual homepage included, lives at [github.com/HotLoop-io](https://github.com/HotLoop-io).
