# HotLoop-io

The legal and brand home for HotLoop. Three things live here, and each is the only copy that counts.

**The license.** [LICENSE.md](./LICENSE.md) holds the HotLoop Source License Agreement 1.0 (SPDX `LicenseRef-HotLoop-Source-1.0`), and every HotLoop product is under it: **HotLoop IoT, HotLoop Edge, HotLoop Gateway, HotLoop Edge Relay, HotLoop Flow, HotLoop Cascade, HotLoop for CODESYS, HotLoop Transmitter, Spark SDK and Spark Manager**. It is source-available and not OSI open source: you may copy, modify and redistribute what you receive, and each version converts to AGPL-3.0 or later four years after it is first made public. Home and lab use is free. Use by accredited schools and educational institutions, nonprofit organizations and government bodies is free too. Business use is free when HotLoop runs through [EmberNet](https://embernet.ai), a partner platform, and hosting HotLoop for your customers is allowed only through EmberNet. Outside EmberNet, hosting HotLoop as a service, reselling it and embedding it in a product you sell need a commercial license. Ask for one at helpdesk@fireballz.ai. Each product moves to the HotLoop Source License Agreement at its next release, and a version already released under an earlier license stays under it.

**The terms.** [TERMS.md](./TERMS.md) is the HotLoop Terms of Service, published at [hotloop.io/terms](https://hotloop.io/terms). It covers how HotLoop is distributed, what we collect (nothing), what we promise and what we don't. Where it and the license disagree about what you may do with the software, the license wins.

**The brand.** [brand/](./brand) holds the design tokens, the logo files, and the written standard for color, type and voice. `brand/tokens.css` is the one copy of the palette that decides anything. [hotloop.io](https://hotloop.io) and [docs.hotloop.io](https://docs.hotloop.io) each vendor a copy, and each one's build fetches this file and fails if its copy has drifted. So change a color here, then copy it over, or neither site deploys. Anything else carrying a copy keeps it in step by hand, so check it when you change one.

Everything else about HotLoop, the actual homepage included, lives at [github.com/HotLoop-io](https://github.com/HotLoop-io).
