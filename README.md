# HotLoop-io

This repo is the legal and brand home for HotLoop. It holds two things.

**The license.** [LICENSE.md](./LICENSE.md) is the HotLoop Community License, and it governs **HotLoop Gateway**. It is source-available, free for individual use, and business use goes through [Embernet](https://embernet.ai). **HotLoop Flow** is a separate product under [Apache-2.0](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0), and it is free for everyone, businesses included. Read the license for the real terms, or read the plain-language version on the [org homepage](https://github.com/HotLoop-io) and at [docs.hotloop.io/licensing.html](https://docs.hotloop.io/licensing.html).

**The brand.** [brand/](./brand) holds the design tokens, the logo files, and the written standard for color, type, and voice. `brand/tokens.css` is meant to be the only copy of the palette that decides anything, and the websites and the products are meant to vendor a copy of it. Nothing enforces that yet, so for now the copies are kept in sync by hand.

Everything else about HotLoop, including the actual homepage, lives at [github.com/HotLoop-io](https://github.com/HotLoop-io).
